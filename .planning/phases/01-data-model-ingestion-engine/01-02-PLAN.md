    - Calculate percentile rank for players with balls_faced >= 30 within their season: `.groupby('season_id')['raw_batting_rating'].transform('rank', pct=True).mul(99).round().astype(int)`.

    - Sub-threshold values default to 50
    - Scaling logic implements exponent of 1.8 and base cost of 4.0
  </acceptance_criteria>
